## Results Summary

### Wild-Type BCKDHA

The wild-type (WT) *BCKDHA* coding sequence from *NM_000709.4* was translated using *Galaxy SeqKit Translate* with reading frame 1 and the standard genetic code.

- *CDS length:* 1,338 bp
- *Predicted protein length:* 445 amino acids
- *Start codon:* ATG
- *Stop codon:* TGA
- *Reading frame:* Frame 1
- *Reference protein:* NP_000700.1

The WT sequence was used as the reference for comparing the documented and artificial mutant sequences.

### Documented Mutation: c.929C>G (p.Thr310Arg)

The documented *BCKDHA c.929C>G* mutation was reproduced by changing nucleotide 929 from *C to G* in a copy of the WT CDS.

This nucleotide substitution changed the codon from *ACA to AGA*, resulting in the replacement of *threonine (T) with arginine (R)* at amino-acid position 310.

- *Mutation type:* Single-nucleotide substitution
- *Protein consequence:* Missense
- *WT protein length:* 445 amino acids
- *Mutant protein length:* 445 amino acids
- *Amino-acid change:* T310R
- *Frameshift:* No
- *Premature stop codon:* No
- *Amino acids inserted/deleted:* None

The documented mutation therefore changed only one amino acid while keeping the reading frame and overall protein length unchanged.

### Artificial Mutation

An artificial single-nucleotide substitution, *c.303G>A*, was introduced into a separate copy of the WT *BCKDHA* CDS. This changed the codon from *AAG to AAA*. Since both codons encode *lysine (K)*, the mutation was predicted to be synonymous.

The translated artificial mutant protein showed no change in the predicted amino-acid sequence compared with the WT protein.

- *Mutation type:* Single-nucleotide substitution
- *Protein consequence:* Synonymous
- *Codon change:* AAG → AAA
- *Amino-acid change:* K → K
- *Protein length:* 445 amino acids
- *Frameshift:* No
- *Premature stop codon:* No
- *Amino-acid changes:* None

### Overall Result

The documented *BCKDHA c.929C>G* mutation produced a single amino-acid substitution, *p.Thr310Arg (T310R)*, while the artificial *c.303G>A* mutation did not change the predicted amino-acid sequence. Both mutant sequences retained the same CDS and protein lengths as the WT sequence and did not produce a frameshift or premature stop codon.

These results show that different single-nucleotide substitutions can have different effects on a protein. A substitution may produce a missense change that alters one amino acid, as seen in the documented BCKDHA mutation, or it may be synonymous and leave the predicted protein sequence unchanged, as observed in the artificial mutation.
